How to fly
Throttle
Drag the lever on the left. Keyboard: Shift / Ctrl or + / −
Stick
Put your thumb anywhere on the right and drag. Down = nose up. Keyboard: arrow keys or WASD
Rudder
Follows the stick automatically and steers the nose or tail wheel on the ground. Q / E for manual rudder
Buttons
Flaps F / V · Gear G · Speed brake U · Brakes B (hold) · Trim T / Y · View C · Map M · Mini map N · Seat belts K · Cabin announcement J · Sound L · Pause P · All keys H
Map
Drag to pan, pinch or +/− to zoom, ⌖ to follow the aircraft again.
Weather
Choose clear skies, scattered clouds, overcast, rain or low fog. Under the clouds it is grey and hazy; climb through the cloud layer and you come out in sunshine above a sea of clouds. Inside the cloud you see almost nothing, so fly by the instruments.
Wind
The windsocks at the fields and the wind turbines show the direction. The runway is chosen to face into the wind.
Bush plane
Tailwheel: lift the tail with a little forward stick and lift off at about 55 kt. Don't brake hard or it will nose over.
A320
Fly-by-wire: let go of the stick and the aircraft holds its flight path and bank. Rotate at about 140 kt and land with flaps FULL at around 135 kt.
Fokker 50
Twin turboprop with classic controls and trim. Take off with flaps 10, rotate at about 105 kt, land with flaps 35 at about 110 kt. Lower the gear in good time, it slows you down too.
Helicopter
With ASSIST on (default) the lever sets climb or descent: the middle (HOLD) keeps the height, up climbs and down descends, up to 2000 ft/min each way, and it flares by itself just above the ground so touchdowns are soft. The stick sets your speed: push forward to go faster, let go and it brakes by itself to a stop and hovers in place, even in wind; pull back to brake harder or back up. Sideways turns the nose in a hover and banks at speed. TRIM ▼/▲ sets a cruise speed it holds when you let go. With ASSIST off you fly it by hand: the lever is the raw collective (about 65% hovers) and the stick tilts the helicopter. Don't sink fast straight down without assist: in a vortex ring the rotor loses lift, so push forward to fly out of it.
Landing
With the landing guide on, pink gates show the glide path to the runway. Steer the green marker (where you are heading) into the pink ring and keep it there, and you fly straight through the gates to the touchdown zone. The pink mark on the speed tape is the speed to fly: it slides down evenly towards landing speed, and the green arrow shows where your speed is heading. Tap A/THR and the throttle flies that speed for you. The line at the bottom tells you when to set flaps and gear. Close to the ground, ease back to flare. Every landing gets up to three stars: soft touchdown, on the centreline and in the touchdown zone.
Autoland
When you're lined up with a runway, tap AUTOLAND (or AP): the autopilot flies the glide path, sets flaps and gear, flares, brakes and stops on the centreline. Move the stick to take over at any time.
Difficulty
Easy (default) levels the wings and flares for you in the last few hundred feet if you come in too steep, and tolerates firmer touchdowns. Realistic leaves it all to you.
